Simulate the airport, before it happens

Plan, rehearse and optimise airport operations with confidence, before committing real resources

Talk to us about a pilot

Every operational change carries risk when the airport is live. Rockport's simulation platform lets you test changes, rehearse busy days, and compare options in a safe, realistic model of your airport first, so decisions are backed by evidence rather than guesswork.

Airside & Terminal Operations

Model stands, gates and terminal layouts before committing real resources. See how a new pier, security layout or stand allocation performs under real traffic before it is built.

Turnaround & Gate Optimisation

Simulate the full turnaround sequence, from arrival to pushback, to find where delays build up and how to close the gap.

Peak Day & Surge Rehearsal

Rehearse your busiest day before it arrives. Model holiday peaks, large events and unplanned surges across the wider transport network.

Passenger Flow & Wait Times

Understand how passengers actually move through check-in, security and gates, and where queues form. Our own Catalyst project demonstrated a 50% reduction in passenger wait times. Hover the image (or tap, on touch devices) to see the model in motion.

Capital Projects & Infrastructure

See the operational impact of a new pier, taxiway or terminal extension before breaking ground, and plan construction phasing around live operations.

Safety & Contingency Rehearsal

Rehearse irregular operations, diversions and emergency scenarios safely, without disrupting a live airport.

Passenger Experience

Follow a single passenger end to end, from kerb to gate, through the same model used to test flow and wait times elsewhere on this page.
