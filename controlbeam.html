ControlBEAM

A common event model for real-time, interoperable digital systems

ControlBEAM is Rockport Software's event-driven framework, named for its Basic Entity Attribute Matrix event model. It gives previously siloed systems a shared, standardised way to exchange real-time information, without custom point-to-point integration.

It is the technical foundation behind our digital twin, simulation, and AI-powered airport work, and behind the Rockport Baggage Message Broker and Aviation Reference Data Service.

Atomic Events

Every system produces and consumes the same standardised, time-stamped unit of information.

Common Ontology

A shared class hierarchy gives every event context, supporting inference and decision making.

Interoperability

Previously siloed systems become interoperable without custom integration work.

Edge to Cloud

A lightweight, portable runtime scales from edge devices to virtual networks in the cloud.

ControlBEAM underpins our AI, simulation and digital twin work, including our airport simulation platform.
